Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You talk to Claude Code in plain English. The kit tells it what to do, and it runs the commands itself.

What do I need besides the kit?+

Claude Code, an ElevenLabs API key for the voice, and Node and ffmpeg on your computer. The render engine is free and open source and is installed during setup. The kit was built and tested on macOS; Windows and Linux haven't been tested yet.

What does each video cost to make?+

There are no video-generation credits. The voice is the only running cost on top of your Claude plan: a 33-second reel used 799 ElevenLabs characters.

Can I use my own script and voice?+

Yes. Paste your script and the agent builds the scenes around it. For the voice, pick any voice in your ElevenLabs account, including a clone of your own, or drop in your own recordings line by line.

Which languages does it work in?+

English is what we've tested. Other languages that use the Latin alphabet should work with a matching ElevenLabs voice, but we haven't tested them yet.

Why $39? Will the price go up?+

$39 is the launch price. When the launch ends the price is $59. It's a one-time payment either way.

Will my videos get views?+

Nobody can honestly promise that. The kit takes care of production, so you can post a clean, well-paced reel every day. The idea and the audience are still yours.
