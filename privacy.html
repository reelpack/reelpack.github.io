Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What we collect

When you buy ReelPack, our payment provider, Dodo Payments, collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to process the order. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can deliver the download and help you if something goes wrong. We never see or store your full card number.

If you email us, we keep your message and email address to answer you.

What this website collects

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and it does not ask you to create an account. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How we use it

Only to deliver your purchase, send order-related emails, answer support requests, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, tax and invoicing. Their own privacy policy applies to the data they process.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support emails are kept for as long as they are useful to help you.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. We will answer within 30 days.

The kit itself

ReelPack runs on your own computer. The files you make with it stay on your computer. The kit does not send your scripts, videos or personal data to us. Third-party tools you connect, such as Claude Code or ElevenLabs, follow their own privacy policies.
